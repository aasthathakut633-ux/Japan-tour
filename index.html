DISCOVER ITS BEAUTY

Welcome to Japan

Japan is an island country in East Asia, famous for its beautiful landscapes, rich history, advanced technology and unique traditions. Its capital city is Tokyo.

From peaceful Buddhist temples and traditional Japanese gardens to colourful city streets and high-speed trains, Japan offers a fascinating combination of old and new.

Japan is also known as the Land of the Rising Sun. Its four beautiful seasons bring cherry blossoms in spring, green landscapes in summer, colourful leaves in autumn and snowy scenery in winter.

EXPLORE BEAUTIFUL DESTINATIONS

Famous Places in Japan

Discover some of the most wonderful destinations in Japan.

Tokyo

Tokyo is Japan's capital and a vibrant city famous for modern skyscrapers, shopping, anime, technology and exciting nightlife.

Kyoto

Kyoto is famous for its historic temples, traditional wooden houses, beautiful gardens and peaceful bamboo forests.

Mount Fuji

Mount Fuji is Japan's iconic mountain. Its beautiful snow-covered peak attracts visitors and photographers from around the world.

Cherry Blossoms

Known as Sakura, cherry blossoms transform Japan into a beautiful pink paradise during the spring season.

TRADITIONS AND HERITAGE

Japanese Culture

Japanese culture is a beautiful combination of traditional customs and modern creativity. Respect, discipline, politeness and harmony are important values in Japanese society.

Popular Japanese Food
